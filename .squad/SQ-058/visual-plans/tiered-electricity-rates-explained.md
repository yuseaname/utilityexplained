## Hero

- **File:** `/images/articles/tiered-electricity-rates-explained/tiered-electricity-rates-explained_hero_00001_.webp` (frontmatter `image:`) | **Viewed:** Flat title-card infographic: slim blue top bar, bold dark headline (Tiered Rates: More Use = Higher Price) over a short blue underline, gray subtitle (Price per kWh rises as you cross each tier); below, three rounded blocks — blue Tier 1 (cheapest, tallest), teal Tier 2 (higher, shortest), amber Tier 3 (highest, middle height) — and a bold footer: Stay under Tier 2 to keep the lowest price per kWh.
- **Verdict: REPLACE.**
  - **(a) Topic relevance:** Fails on the math it shows. The bars claim price rises by tier, but bar height encodes nothing honest: Tier 1 is drawn tallest while being cheapest, Tier 3 (highest price) is shorter than Tier 1 — a chart that visually contradicts its own title. The footer (Stay under Tier 2) is a generic rule of exactly the kind the Quick Answer bans (do not apply a generic baseline…), and the illustration predates the page's own worked example (400/550 kWh, $0.28/$0.38), which it neither depicts nor matches.
  - **(b) Style consistency:** Off-bible deprecated title-card template (same family as the 08 hero): saturated blue that is not in the ledger palette, block-rectangle composition, non-house typography, no grid-teal scaffolding, no mono figures, no amber reserved for the one line that matters (here amber is decoration on Tier 3).
  - **(c) Suitability:** Unsalvageable — the wrong bars would need redrawing into what the body already computes. **Replace with a flat-editorial scene in the trio's family grammar:** same ink-teal/cream/amber flat-vector register and ≈3:2 ratio as the kept 49 hero, pointed at how much — the house with two columnar usage bins beside the meter: the first filled to a marked threshold line (amber line, mono `400 kWh` label), the overflow spilling into a second taller bin labeled `+ $0.38/kWh`; auditor annotation: crossing the line prices only what spills. Proposed file: `static/images/articles/tiered-electricity-rates-explained/tier-usage-bins-hero.webp`; frontmatter `image:` updated at implementation.
