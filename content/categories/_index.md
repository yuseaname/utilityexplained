## Browse by Category

Every guide on this site belongs to one of the topic categories below. Each category page gathers the full guide list for that subject — rates, bill-reading, troubleshooting, and savings for that utility.

If you'd rather start from a problem than a category, the [blog directory](/blog/) organizes the same guides by what brought you here ("why is my bill high", "how do I read this line"), and the pillar pages — [electricity explained](/electricity-explained/), [gas explained](/gas-explained/), [water explained](/water-explained/), [heating and cooling explained](/heating-cooling-explained/) — each open with the fundamentals before linking into the deep guides.
