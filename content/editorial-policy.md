# Editorial Policy

This page explains how Utility Explained creates and maintains its content. Our goal is simple: every guide should help a real person understand and act on a utility-bill question.

## What we cover

Plain-English explainers on electricity, gas, and water bills — how to read them, why they change, what every charge means, and how to lower them. We focus on the **informational and problem-solving** questions bill-payers actually have.

## What we don't do

- We do **not** provide financial, legal, or professional advice. Content is for educational purposes (see our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/)).
- We do **not** sell products or services, and we do **not** accept payment to recommend providers.
- We do **not** require user accounts or collect sensitive personal data (see our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/)).

## How we write

- **Plain English.** Technical terms (kWh, CCF, therms) are defined on first use and cross-linked to our [glossary](/utilities-glossary/).
- **Lead with the answer.** Guides open with a direct answer to the question, then add the supporting detail.
- **Numbers over vagueness.** We use typical U.S. cost ranges and label them as estimates. See our [methodology](/methodology/).
- **No fear or hype.** Bills cause enough anxiety; our job is to resolve it with clarity, not amplify it.

## AI assistance

Our guides may be drafted with AI assistance. That assistance does not establish a fact, source, credential, or recommendation. Data-heavy guides are published or refreshed only when they show direct, dated source links for their material figures and claims. We are reviewing older guides against that standard; a page without a visible Sources section should be treated as general educational context, not a current rate quote, legal determination, or personalized advice. We do not present AI-generated credentials or fabricated expert qualifications. See our [methodology](/methodology/) for how we source figures.

## Advertising

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Ad placements are automated and editorially independent — advertising never influences which topics we cover or what we say about them. See our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for details.

## Review and freshness

Guides are revisited to keep figures and rate references current. Material updates carry a visible "Updated" date.

## Accountability

We stand behind a public [corrections policy](/corrections/). If you find an error, [contact us](/contact/) and we will fix it.
