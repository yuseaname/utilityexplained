# Corrections Policy

Accuracy matters when the topic is your money. If we get something wrong, we want to fix it — fast and visibly.

## How to report an error

If you spot an inaccuracy — a wrong figure, a misleading explanation, an outdated rate — please [contact us](/contact/) with the guide title and the specific issue. The more detail you provide, the faster we can verify and correct it.

## What happens next

1. We review the claim against our [sources and methodology](/methodology/).
2. If the content is wrong, we correct it promptly.
3. Where a figure was materially off, we update the "Updated" date so the change is visible.
4. For significant corrections, we note what changed so readers aren't confused by a silent edit.

## Updates vs. corrections

- A **correction** fixes something that was inaccurate.
- An **update** refreshes a figure or rate to keep it current (e.g., new average prices). Updates carry an "Updated" timestamp.

Both are part of keeping the guides trustworthy. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for how we maintain content over time.

Thank you for helping us stay accurate.
