# Contact Utility Explained

Have a utility-bill question we haven't answered? Found an error? Want to suggest a topic? We read every message.

## Before you reach out

We may already have your answer:

- **Searching for a term?** Check the [glossary](/utilities-glossary/).
- **Looking for a specific guide?** [Search all guides](/search/).
- **Found a wrong number?** See our [corrections policy](/corrections/).

## Send us a message

The quickest way to reach us is by email. Your email app will open with our address pre-filled:

<a href="mailto:hello@utilityexplained.com?subject=Utility%20Explained%20%E2%80%94%20Reader%20Message" class="hero-meter-cta" style="display:inline-flex;">Email hello@utilityexplained.com</a>

## What to expect

This is an informational contact — it does **not** create a customer-support relationship with any utility provider, and we cannot look up your personal account or bill. We consider reader questions for future guides and correct factual errors promptly.

We can't always reply individually, but every message helps us improve the site.
