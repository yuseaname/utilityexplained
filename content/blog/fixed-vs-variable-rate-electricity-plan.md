## Quick Answer

**A fixed or variable label does not by itself tell you the total cost of an electricity offer.** Compare the complete written terms, every charge, the account's current tariff, and your own historical usage before enrolling.

DOE notes that utilities and third-party providers may offer time-variable pricing, and that actual rate options depend on the service area and customer class.[1][2]

## What to Compare

Request the current offer and record:

| Item | Question to answer |
|---|---|
| Availability | Is this offer available for my account and address? |
| Price rule | Is the price fixed, indexed, variable, or time-variable? |
| Term | What start, end, renewal, and notice terms apply? |
| Charges | Which supply, delivery, tax, rider, customer, and one-time charges apply? |
| Exit terms | What cancellation or switching conditions are written into the offer? |
| Usage basis | How does the offer apply to my own kWh and billing pattern? |

Do not assume that delivery charges, contract length, deposit requirements, or renewal behavior will match an example from another provider or service territory.

## Use Your Own History

Collect several comparable bills and identify kWh, billing days, rate-plan name, fixed charges, and seasonal or time-variable usage if available. Apply the written offer only to the portions of the bill it actually changes.

The lowest advertised supply price is not necessarily the lowest full bill. Compare like-for-like totals and keep the contract version used for the comparison.

## Questions Before Enrolling

1. Which exact tariff or contract section defines the price?
2. What charges are outside the advertised energy price?
3. What change, renewal, cancellation, and dispute terms apply?
4. Can the provider calculate the offer using my historical usage?
5. Which agency or consumer office oversees this offer in my service area?

If the offer is incomplete or terms conflict with the provider's written materials, do not enroll until the provider or local regulator resolves the discrepancy.
