## Quick Answer

**Charging an electric vehicle at home typically adds $30-60 to your monthly electric bill (approx. $0.04-0.06 per mile), about half the cost of gas for similar mileage.** Most EV owners see a 15-30% increase in total household electricity consumption.

- **Home charging cost: ~$500-800 annually vs. $1,200-1,800 for gas**
- **Time-of-use charging can save $100-200 per year**

[For more detail, see our guide to [electricity rate plans](/blog/time-of-use-electricity-rates/).]

You're considering an electric vehicle (EV). You've heard that "charging at home is cheaper than gas," but how much will it actually increase your electric bill? Will it erase your savings? Will it push you into a higher rate tier?

The answer depends on three things: how much you drive, when you charge, and your electric rate. For most EV owners, home charging adds $40–$80/month to the electric bill—but saves $100–$180/month on gas.

Here's the complete math, how to charge during off-peak hours to save even more, and whether an EV makes financial sense for your situation.

## The EV Charging Math

To calculate how much EV charging will cost, you need three numbers:
1. **Miles driven per month**
2. **EV efficiency (miles per kWh)**
3. **Electricity rate (¢/kWh)**

### Step 1: How Much Do You Drive?

| Driving Profile | Miles/Year | Miles/Month |
|-----------------|------------|-------------|
| **Light** | 6,000 | 500 |
| **Average** | 12,000 | 1,000 |
| **Heavy** | 18,000 | 1,500 |
| **Commuter** | 24,000 | 2,000 |

**Average American:** 13,500 miles/year (1,125 miles/month)

---

### Step 2: How Efficient Is Your EV?

| EV Model | Miles Per kWh | kWh per 100 Miles | Monthly kWh (at 1,000 miles/month) |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Tesla Model 3** | 4.2 | 24 | 240 |
| **Tesla Model Y** | 3.8 | 26 | 260 |
| **Chevy Bolt** | 3.5 | 29 | 290 |
| **Ford F-150 Lightning** | 2.0 | 50 | 500 |
| **Rivian R1T** | 1.8 | 56 | 560 |

**The math:** Miles driven ÷ miles per kWh = kWh used for charging

---

### Step 3: What's Your Electricity Rate?

| Rate Type | Typical Rate | Best For EV Owners? |
|-----------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Flat Rate** | 12–15¢/kWh | Okay (simple but no savings) |
| **Tiered Rate** | 10–20¢/kWh | Risky (EV can push you into higher tier) |
| **TOU - Off-Peak** | 8–12¢/kWh | Best (lowest rate if you charge at night) |
| **TOU - Peak** | 20–40¢/kWh | Worst (avoid charging during peak) |

## Off-Peak Charging: How to Save

If you're on a TOU (time-of-use) rate plan, WHEN you charge matters as much as how much you charge.

### TOU Rate Structure (Example)

| Time Period | Hours | Rate |
|-------------|-------|------|
| **Off-Peak** | 9pm–9am weekdays, all weekend | 8–12¢/kWh |
| **Mid-Peak** | 9am–4pm weekdays | 14–18¢/kWh |
| **Peak** | 4pm–9pm weekdays | 28–40¢/kWh |

**The math:** Charging off-peak costs 70–80% less than charging during peak hours.

---

### TOU Charging Costs by Time (Tesla Model 3 Example)

| Charging Time | Rate | Monthly Cost (238 kWh) |
|---------------|------|------------------------|
| **Off-Peak (overnight)** | 10¢/kWh | $23.80 |
| **Mid-Peak (workday)** | 16¢/kWh | $38.08 |
| **Peak (afternoon)** | 38¢/kWh | $90.44 |

**Savings by charging off-peak:** Up to **$66/month** vs. peak charging

## EV vs Gas: Cost Comparison

### Monthly Fuel Cost Comparison

**Scenario:** 1,000 miles/month driven

| Vehicle Type | Fuel/Energy Cost | Monthly Cost | Annual Cost |
|--------------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Gas car (25 MPG)** | $3.50/gallon | $140 | $1,680 |
| **Gas car (30 MPG)** | $3.50/gallon | $117 | $1,404 |
| **Gas car (35 MPG)** | $3.50/gallon | $100 | $1,200 |
| **EV (Tesla Model 3, flat rate)** | 14¢/kWh | $33 | $396 |
| **EV (Tesla Model 3, TOU off-peak)** | 10¢/kWh | $24 | $288 |

**Savings:** $93–$116/month vs. 25 MPG gas car

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will charging an EV increase my electric bill?

**Yes, but less than you'd spend on gas.**

**Typical increase:** $30–$80/month for average driving
**Gas savings:** $80–$150/month

**Net effect:** Most EV owners save $50–$100/month on fuel costs.

---

### Can my existing electrical panel handle an EV charger?

**Maybe.** Here's how to check:

| Panel Capacity | Can Support Level 2 Charger? |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| 100 amps | Maybe (need available capacity) |
| 150 amps | Yes (usually) |
| 200 amps | Yes (easily) |

### Is it cheaper to charge at home or public chargers?

**Home charging is almost always cheaper.**

| Charging Location | Typical Rate | Cost to Charge 50 kWh |
|-------------------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Home (flat rate)** | 12–15¢/kWh | $6–$7.50 |
| **Home (TOU off-peak)** | 8–12¢/kWh | $4–$6 |
| **Public Level 2** | 20–30¢/kWh | $10–$15 |
| **DC Fast Charging** | 30–50¢/kWh | $15–$25 |
