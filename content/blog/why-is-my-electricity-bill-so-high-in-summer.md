## Quick Answer

Summer electric bills spike because air conditioning drives **40–50%** of home energy use during hot months. The biggest cost-cutting levers: raise the thermostat above **78°F**, shift laundry and dishes to off-peak hours, and seal duct leaks. Together these can cut cooling costs by **15–30%**.
