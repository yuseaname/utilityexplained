## Quick Answer

**There is no nationwide cheapest hour for electricity.** The applicable hours, prices, seasons, holidays, enrollment rules, and charges come from your account's current utility tariff and rate plan. DOE describes time-variable rates that can change by set amounts at set times and other rates that can reflect hour-to-hour market movements.[1][2]

Do not schedule appliances or switch plans using a generic peak/off-peak timetable. First verify that your account has time-based pricing and obtain its written terms.

## Identify the Current Rate Plan

Use the latest statement, online account, or provider to record:

| Item | What to verify |
|---|---|
| Rate-plan name | Exact tariff, service class, and effective date |
| Time periods | Hours, days, seasons, holidays, and time zone |
| Price calculation | Usage rate, demand component if applicable, fixed charges, riders, and taxes |
| Enrollment | Default, optional, opt-out, or contract rules |
| Usage data | Whether interval data is available and how it is measured |

If the bill does not show a time-based component, do not assume moving use to a particular hour changes the usage price.

## Compare Your Own Usage and Bill

Ask the provider for interval data or an account-specific rate comparison if available. Compare the same billing period and include all bill components, not only an advertised energy rate. A time-based plan can produce a different result depending on when and how the household uses electricity.

Check manufacturer instructions and safety requirements before using timers, delayed-start features, charging schedules, or automated controls. Never schedule a device in a way that conflicts with its operating instructions or property safety rules.

## Questions for the Provider

1. Which tariff and time-period schedule apply to my account today?
2. Are there seasonal, weekend, holiday, or demand-charge rules?
3. Can I obtain interval-use data and a full-bill comparison for another plan?
4. What enrollment, cancellation, or rate-change terms apply?
5. Which charges remain the same regardless of time of use?
