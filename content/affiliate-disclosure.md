Utility Explained is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you.

## How we make money

Our primary revenue is display advertising. On product-focused guides, we also use **affiliate links** — primarily the Amazon Associates program. If you click one of those links and buy something, Amazon pays us a small commission. The price you pay is exactly the same.

**As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.**

## What this means for you

- We only recommend products we believe genuinely help you understand, manage, or lower your utility bills.
- A commission never determines what we cover or what we say about a product.
- Where we see a cheaper or better path that doesn't earn us anything, we'll say so — most of our content is free advice that requires no purchase at all.
- Affiliate links are marked as "Check price on Amazon" buttons or noted near the link.

## Why gadgets at all

Some utility problems are solved with a $25 smart plug or a $40 leak detector — not a plumber or an electrician. When a product honestly saves money on a water, gas, or electric bill, it belongs in the conversation.
