# Link Magnet Outreach Templates — 2026 Utility Cost Index

This file contains three personalized outreach email templates for promoting the [2026 Utility Cost Index by State](/blog/2026-utility-cost-index-by-state) to personal finance bloggers, energy/solar websites, and moving/relocation sites. Each template is designed for a different audience and should be customized with the recipient's name, publication, and a specific reference to their recent work.

Before sending:
- Always send from a real person's name (not a generic inbox).
- Personalize at least two sentences per email — a recent article, a shared contact, a topic you both cover.
- Keep the email under 200 words. Editors are busy.
- Include the data link clearly, not buried.
- Follow up once, no more than twice.
