# Featured Snippet Optimization Guide

## Target Featured Snippets (10 Winnable Opportunities)

### 1. "What is kWh"
A kilowatt hour (kWh) is **the standard unit for measuring electricity usage**. It represents **the energy used by a 1,000-watt appliance running for one hour**.

### 2. "How to read electric meter"
1. Locate your meter (exterior wall or basement)
2. Identify type (digital or analog)
3. Read left to right
4. For analog: if pointer between numbers, use lower number
5. Calculate: Current - Previous = kWh used

### 3. "What is CCF on water bill"
**CCF** = Centum Cubic Feet = **100 cubic feet** = **748 gallons**
Average household: 3-5 CCF/month

### 4. "Delivery charge vs supply charge"
| Delivery (Distribution) | Supply (Generation) |
|------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Cost to DELIVER electricity | Cost to GENERATE electricity |
| Goes to utility company | Goes to suppliers |
| Covers: lines, poles, meters | Covers: power plants, fuel |
| **Cannot switch** | **CAN shop around** |

### 5. "What is a therm gas bill"
**1 therm** = 100,000 BTUs = ~100 cubic feet natural gas
Average: 20-40 therms/month (summer), 80-150 therms/month (winter)
