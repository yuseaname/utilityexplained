## Platform
Reddit - r/homeowners

## Question
Smart meter installed, bill went up 25%. Is this normal?

## Answer
Common concern. What's probably happening: Your old analog meter was under-recording (mechanical wear slows them by 5-15% over 15-20 years). Smart meters are digital and 99.9% accurate. Reality: You were being UNDERCHARGED before, now paying correctly. Also check: Timing coincidence (install during high usage months), Rate changes coinciding with install, Seasonal factors. To verify: Request 'meter accuracy test' from utility (often free). Errors happen but are rare (<1%). Most cases are old meter being slow, not new meter being fast.
