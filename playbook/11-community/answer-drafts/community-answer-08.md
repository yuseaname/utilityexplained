## Question
What does CCF mean on my water bill?

## Answer
CCF = Centum Cubic Feet = standard unit for water consumption. 1 CCF = 100 cubic feet = 748 gallons. Utilities use CCF to keep numbers smaller. Typical usage: 1 person = 2-3 CCF/month, family of 4 = 6-10 CCF/month. Quick calculation: Multiply CCF by 748 to get total gallons. If seems excessive, you probably have a leak. Most common: Running toilets.
