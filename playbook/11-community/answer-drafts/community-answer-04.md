## Platform
Reddit - r/electricians

## Question
Customer asking about demand charges on residential bill

## Answer
Most residential bills DON'T have demand charges, but becoming more common with TOU plans. Demand charges based on peak 15-minute usage. Where to check: Look for 'Demand Charge' or anything in kW not kWh. Spike usually from AC + dryer + oven running simultaneously. Solution: Shift high-draw activities to different times.
