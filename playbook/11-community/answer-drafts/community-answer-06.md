## Question
AC running all day, electric bill through the roof

## Answer
Yes, this is normal for extended AC use. Central AC (3-ton): ~3-3.5 kWh/hour. Heat wave: 12-16 hours/day = 36-56 kWh/day. At $0.15/kWh: $162-252 just for AC. Tips: Set thermostat to 78°F (saves 5-15%), use ceiling fans, close blinds, change filter (dirty adds 5-15%). If AC over 15 years old, new unit pays for itself in 5-7 years.
