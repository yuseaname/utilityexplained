## Platform
Reddit - r/energy

## Question
Time-of-use rates worth switching to?

## Answer
TOU can save OR cost money — depends on lifestyle. Who benefits: EV owners charging overnight, shift workers, flexible schedules. Who loses: Stay-at-home parents, rigid schedules. If <30% of usage during peak, TOU probably saves. If >50% during peak, stay on flat rates. Most people can't shift enough usage to make TOU worth it.
