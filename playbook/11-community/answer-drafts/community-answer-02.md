## Question
Best ways to lower water bill?

## Answer
High-ROI fixes under $50: Low-flow showerhead ($15-25, saves 20-30%), fix dripping faucets (one drip/second = 2,000 gallons/year), toilet dye test for leaks. Behavior: shorter showers (5 min vs 20 min = 10-15 vs 40-60 gallons), only full loads. Average person uses 80-100 gallons/day. Family of 4: 8,000-12,000 gallons/month is normal.
