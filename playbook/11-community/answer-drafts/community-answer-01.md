Reddit - r/personalfinance

## Question
My electric bill doubled from $120 to $240. What could cause this?

## Answer
This happened to me. Compare kWh usage (not dollars) to same month last year. If kWh similar but bill doubled → rates. If kWh doubled → usage. Most common: HVAC issues (dirty filter adds 5-15%), water heater failures, or rate plan changes. In my case, failing AC was the culprit. Replaced it and bill dropped.
