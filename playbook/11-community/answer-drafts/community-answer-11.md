## Question
How does net metering show up on my electric bill?

## Answer
Net metering makes bills look different. You'll see: Generation credit (what panels produced = negative usage), Net usage (consumption minus generation), Minimum charge ($10-20 even if generating 100%). Example: Consumed 800 kWh, panels generated -600 kWh, net 200 kWh. Supply: 200 × $0.08 = $16, Delivery: 200 × $0.045 = $9, Minimum: $15 = Total $40 (vs $135 without solar). Solar typically cuts bills 60-90%, not 100% because minimum fee is unavoidable.
