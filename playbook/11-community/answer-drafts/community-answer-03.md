Reddit - r/homeowners

## Question
Can someone explain delivery vs supply charges?

## Answer
Delivery covers infrastructure (lines, poles, meters) — fixed by utility, can't change. Supply is actual electricity — competitive in many states. Example (1,000 kWh): Supply $80 + Delivery $45 + Customer $10 = $135. If in deregulated state (PA, OH, TX, etc.), shopping for supply saves 10-25% typically.
