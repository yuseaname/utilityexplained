## Question
Landlord charging me for utilities, how do I verify?

## Answer
In most states, if paying utilities directly to landlord, you have right to see actual bills. Request: Copies of utility bills, formula for your portion, proof submetering is legal. Red flags: Flat monthly fees, refusing to show bills, charges higher than shown. To estimate: 1BR apartment uses ~600-800 kWh/month. Keep requests in writing.
