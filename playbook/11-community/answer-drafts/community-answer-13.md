## Question
Phantom loads are real — here's how much they actually cost

## Answer
Phantom power is absolutely real. What uses power when 'off': TVs 1-5 watts = $1-5/year, Cable boxes 15-30 watts = $15-30/year (worst offender), Computers 2-5 watts sleep = $2-5/year. Real hogs: Old refrigerators 100-150 kWh/year = $100-150/year, Second freezers 200-400 kWh/year = $200-300/year. Total phantom: 5-10% of bill = $75-150/year. Worth fixing: Smart power strips ($20-30, saves $20-50/year), Unloading second fridge (saves $150-300/year). Low priority: Unplugging phone chargers (saves <$0.50/year).
