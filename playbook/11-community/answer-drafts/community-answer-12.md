## Question
Buying first home, how much should I budget for utilities?

## Answer
Typical monthly: Apartment (<1,000 sq ft) = $150-250, Small home (1,000-1,500 sq ft) = $250-350, Average home (1,500-2,500 sq ft) = $350-500, Large home (2,500+ sq ft) = $500-800. By climate: Mild = $200-350, Cold winters = $350-600, Hot summers = $350-550. Breakdown: Electricity $100-200, Gas $50-150 (winter: $100-250), Water $40-80, Trash $20-40. Pro tip: Ask seller for past 12 months of bills.
