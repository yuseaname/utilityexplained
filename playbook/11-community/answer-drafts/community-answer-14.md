Quora

## Question
Why is my gas bill higher in summer when I'm not using heat?

## Answer
Several reasons gas bills stay high in summer: Gas water heating (15-20% of annual usage even in summer), Gas dryer (5 loads/week = 0.75-1.25 therms/month), Gas stove/oven (0.5-1 therms/month), Gas fireplace pilot light (0.5 therms/month = $5-10/month year-round), Pool heater (20-50 therms/month). Typical summer: Without pool = 10-20 therms/month, With pool = 30-60 therms/month. If unusually high, check for gas leaks.
