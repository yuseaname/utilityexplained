## Question
What is fuel adjustment charge on electric bill?

## Answer
Fuel Adjustment Charge (FAC) is a pass-through charge fluctuating based on utility's fuel costs. Covers: Fuel for power plants, purchased power, transmission costs. Base rate stays stable. FAC changes monthly/quarterly. Utilities don't profit from this (direct pass-through). When fuel prices go up, this charge increases. If FAC is 30%+ of total bill, that's unusually high.
