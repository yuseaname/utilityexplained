## Question
How many kWh does average household use per month?

## Answer
National average: ~886 kWh/month (~29 kWh/day). Range: 600-1,200 kWh/month depending on climate. Summer (South): 1,000-1,500 kWh (AC). Winter (Northeast): 800-1,200 kWh (heating). By household size: 1-2 people = 500-700 kWh, 3-4 people = 800-1,000 kWh, 5+ people = 1,000-1,400 kWh. What drives usage: HVAC (45-50%), water heating (15-20%), lighting (10-15%), appliances (20-25%).
