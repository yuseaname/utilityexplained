# Atomization: What Is a Kilowatt Hour

## 1. Twitter/X Thread
Understanding kWh takes 2 minutes and saves you hundreds. Here's the math 🧵

1/6 1 kWh = 1,000 watts for 1 hour. Like running a 100W bulb for 10 hours.

2/6 Space heater math: 1,500W × 4 hours = 6 kWh = $0.90 per use.

3/6 Old fridge costs $100-150/year more than it should.

4/6 Central AC uses 3-3.5 kWh/hour = $300-500/month if run constantly.

5/6 Average U.S. household: 886 kWh/month (~29 kWh/day).

6/6 Full guide with appliance cheat sheet: https://utilityexplained.com/blog/what-is-a-kilowatt-hour/
